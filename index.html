Travel To Disney🪄❤️💛

When you arrive to disney you will first be in shock about how nice and beautiful it looks. When I first got to disney it felt like a dream come true, the food was amazing, the hotel was beautiful, AND at night the castle looked so coloful with all the lights on it.

What To Expect Tip #1:

  • All types of disney characters, you should try to dress up as them👠
  • Tasty Food that represents the disney idea, mickey-shaped waffles, corn dogs, and so much more🍍
  • Cool, Fast, and some scary rides that end up becoming enjoyable afterwards🥳

Things To Do Tip #2:

Obviously when you first wake up you have to get ready to start your day, you will then have breakfast🧇. Afterwards, you can start making your way to the theme park🎡. When you get there the lines are super LONG, but its okay you can walk around and get an idea of the rides/food spots there. Beyond the rides, you can enjoy vibrant entertainment like the nightly Happily Ever After fireworks spectacular at the Magic Kingdom, or relax away from the crowds at the Typhoon Lagoon water park. To round out your trip, you can head to Disney Springs for an array of outdoor shopping, diverse dining options, and unique experiences like the Aerophile balloon flight

Who You Should Go With Tip #3:

You should always go with people you know you can have fun with. When I first went to disney I went with my cheerleading team back in 2024 and I had so much fun😊. Next time I go to disney I really want to go with my family because everything with family makes it even more exciting. I might go with my friends for a girls trip🥰.
