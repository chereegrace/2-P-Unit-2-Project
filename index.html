Travel To Disney🪄❤️💛

When you arrive to disney you will first be in shock about how nice and beautiful it looks. When I first got to disney it felt like a dream come true, the food was amazing, the hotel was beautiful, AND at night the castle looked so coloful with all the lights on it.

What To Expect Tip #1:

  • All types of disney characters, you should try to dress up as them👠
  • Tasty Food that represents the disney idea, mickey-shaped waffles, corn dogs, and so much more🍍
  • Cool, Fast, and some scary rides that end up becoming enjoyable afterwards🥳

Things To Do Tip #2:

Obviously when you first wake up you have to get ready to start your day, you will then have breakfast🧇. Afterwards, you can start making your way to the theme park🎡. When you get there the lines are super LONG, but it's okay you can walk around and get an idea of the rides/food spots there.

Who You Should Go With Tip #3:

You should always go with people you know you can have fun with. When I first went to disney I went with my cheerleading team back in 2024 and I had so much fun😊. Next time I go to disney I really want to go with my family because everything with family makes it even more exciting. I also might go with my friends for a little girls trip or for my birthday and have DISNEY fun🥰.
